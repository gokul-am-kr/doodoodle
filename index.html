The idea behind it all

You already know how to begin.

Doo Doodle began with a simple scribble after a long day at a screen. One circle became another line, then an idea. That small moment of quiet grew into a way to create without pressure.

We break things down into simple shapes, add personality, explore colour, and make room for your own imagination. You don't need to draw “well” to enjoy drawing.

Why doodle?

Five minutes that feel like yours.

A small daily practice can give you a welcome pause from screens and a playful way to focus. Some people doodle to unwind; others discover new ways to think and make.

✎

A screen break

Put your phone down and make something with your hands, even for a few minutes.

◡

Room to breathe

No grades or perfect outcomes. Let the line go where it wants to go.

✦

Fresh ideas

Look at an ordinary shape again and see what else it could become.

A little practice. A lot of discovery.

Good to know

Questions before you begin?

We've gathered the things most new doodlers ask.

Do I need any drawing experience?

None. The course begins with basic marks and shapes. Bring a pen and your curiosity.

Is this for children and adults?

Yes. The new course is designed around shared lessons with room to adapt each activity to a learner's age and interests. Younger children may enjoy drawing alongside an adult.

What materials do I need?

Pen and paper are enough to start. You can add markers or other colours as the lessons progress.

How long is the course?

The planned course has 21 lessons, ending with a handmade greeting card. Detailed timing will be announced with enrolment.

Is there a free way to try it?

Register for the free live webinar. You'll make a doodle with Gokul and get a feel for the approach.

What happens after I enrol?

After payment is confirmed, we’ll contact you on WhatsApp with access to the current course, resources and community. The new 21-lesson edition is in preparation.

When can I get the workbook?

The illustrated workbook is in development. Email us for updates and a preview when it is ready.
